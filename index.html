Hong Kong Transit Dashboard

Hong Kong Octopus Gate System

Tap the Octopus card to enter an immersive MTR operations dashboard.

Purpose

Fast Throughput Cashless Transit Automated Records

Enable fast, automated, cashless fare collection to reduce queues and improve efficiency.

Manual Cash Payment

  • Queue to buy or prepare exact fare
  • Slower passenger flow at gates
  • More manual handling by staff

Automated Octopus System

  • Quick tap-and-go experience
  • Fast passenger movement
  • Automatic fare deduction and records

Calculates fare and updates transaction records.
